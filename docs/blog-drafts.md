## 1. CRM or spreadsheet? How to decide for a growing Tamil Nadu business

A spreadsheet is a fine CRM for a while. It stops being fine at a predictable
point, and you can spot it before it costs you a customer.

**A spreadsheet still works when**
- One or two people touch the data.
- You can remember every open deal without looking.
- Nobody asks "who spoke to this customer last?"

**Move to a CRM when**
- Two people have called the same customer in the same week.
- Follow-ups depend on someone remembering, not on a reminder.
- You can't say how many deals are open, or what they're worth, without an
  afternoon of counting.
- A salesperson leaves and their pipeline leaves with them.

**What to ask before you buy or build**
1. Who owns each deal, and what happens when they're away?
2. What are your real pipeline stages? Name them in your own words, not the
   tool's defaults.
3. Which systems must it talk to — billing, WhatsApp, email, accounts?
4. Who will keep it clean after we hand it over?

A CRM that matches how you already sell gets used. One that forces a new process
gets abandoned within a month. That is why we tailor the pipeline stages and
ownership rules instead of shipping a fixed template.

## 2. How to choose a software company in Udumalpet, Tiruppur or Coimbatore

Most software projects go wrong at the start, not the end. Before you sign with
anyone, check these.

1. **Written scope before code.** You should see a plan, an estimate and a named
   owner before development starts.
2. **Who actually builds it.** Ask who will write the code and whether you can
   talk to them, not only to sales.
3. **How quality is checked.** "We'll test it" isn't an answer. Ask what gets
   tested, by whom, and what you see.
4. **What you own at the end.** Source code, hosting accounts, domain and
   documentation should be yours.
5. **Handover.** Ask what happens after launch: who fixes bugs, and how fast.
6. **Proof you can verify.** Ask to see a live product or speak to a past client.
   If a company can't offer either, treat that as information.
7. **Distance.** Local helps for meetings, but most work happens over calls. Pick
   on process and communication, not postcode alone.

produce, and what we'd advise you not to build yet.

## 3. What custom software costs in India, and what changes the price

We won't quote a number here, because the honest answer depends on a few things
you can name in an hour.

**What moves the price most**
- **Scope.** The number of screens, user roles and rules. Every "and also" adds
  work.
- **Integrations.** Connecting payments, WhatsApp, accounts or an existing system
  usually costs more than the screens themselves.
- **Platforms.** Web only, or web plus Android and iOS.
- **Data.** Moving old data across and cleaning it is often under-estimated.
- **Compliance and security.** Payments, personal data and audits add real work.
- **Speed.** A hard deadline needs more people at once.

**What cuts cost without cutting quality**
- Build the smallest useful version first.
- Decide what you will not build yet.
- Give one person the authority to say yes or no.

**How to compare quotes**
Ask each company the same questions: what's included, what isn't, what changes
the price later, and what you own at the end. Two quotes are only comparable if
the scope is written down the same way.

A short discovery phase is the cheapest way to turn "how much?" into a number you
can trust.
